Journal and Friends

The Journal is your own record: what you cooked, when, from which exact version, and a note for next time that only you see. Friends is the same cooks from people you approved, newest first. Each cook has its own audience, Only me or Friends, chosen before it is posted. Every person in the feed can be muted, reported or blocked from the entry itself.

A · Friends feed: a linked recipe, an improvised dinner, a cook from a private recipe, an external source with no photo. Photography leads; counts stay small. A private recipe's cook shows the dish, never a link or preview. A source cook links out and never copies the publisher's text. The ⋯ menu is open on one entry.
