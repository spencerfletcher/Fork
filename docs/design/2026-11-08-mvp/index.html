Fork MVP design, 8 November

Mockups for the next build: the recipe page and Cook first, then the editor, then a lightweight Journal and Friends feed. Built on the shipped tokens and class names; open each at phone and desktop width. Notes and the decisions that need Spencer are in DESIGN-MVP.md.

  1. Recipe overview

    Cook leads the actions, practical facts sit above counts, named ingredient and method groups, yield beside servings, no-photo and minimal states.

  2. Cook view

    The band collapses to one strip so the working recipe starts at the top; the method ends with a prefilled “Log this cook”.

  3. Editor: groups, yield, audience

    Groups typed as # lines with a read-back of how Fork split them, an optional Makes field, Only me or Public. New, editing and blocked-save states.

  4. Journal and Friends

    Your dated cooking record with private notes, a chronological friends feed with mute, report and block, the log form, and both empty states.
