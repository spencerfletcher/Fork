### 1. `cardamom-cinnamon-buns`

- **Title:** Cardamom Cinnamon Buns with Brown Butter Glaze
- **Owner:** Maria · **Visibility:** Public · **Photo:** p3
- **Description:** Soft enriched buns with cardamom in the dough and the filling, finished with a nutty brown butter glaze while still warm.
- **Measure:** `45 min prep · 25 min cook · Serves 12 · Makes 12 buns`
- **Tags:** baking, breakfast, weekend
- **Stats:** 2 adaptations · 14 saves · 3 versions
- **Versions:**
  - `v3` Sat 24 Oct: "Brown butter glaze instead of cream cheese icing"
  - `v2` Sun 11 Oct: "More cardamom in the dough, 1 tsp to 1½ tsp"
  - `v1` Sat 26 Sep: "First bake"
- **Adaptations:** "Cardamom Buns, Less Sweet" by `@devcooks` (from v2); "Vegan Cardamom Buns" by `@samlind` (from v3)
- **Personal line (Maria):** You cooked this on Sun 8 Nov · Log a cook

**Ingredients**

Dough
- 1 cup (240 ml) whole milk, warmed
- 7 g instant yeast
- ¼ cup (50 g) caster sugar
- 1 large egg
- 2⅓ cups (295 g) bread flour
- ¾ tsp fine salt
- 1½ tsp ground cardamom
- ¼ cup (55 g) unsalted butter, softened

Filling
- ½ cup (110 g) unsalted butter, softened
- ⅔ cup (130 g) light brown sugar
- 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp ground cardamom
- pinch of salt

Glaze
- ¼ cup (55 g) unsalted butter
- 1½ cups (180 g) icing sugar
- 2 tbsp milk
- ½ tsp vanilla extract

**Method** (steps number straight through the groups; method groups are their own, not the ingredient groups)

Dough
1. Warm the milk to about 40 °C, whisk in the yeast and sugar, and leave for 5 minutes until foamy.
2. Add the egg, flour, salt and cardamom. Knead for 8 minutes, then work in the butter a piece at a time and knead 5 minutes more, until smooth and elastic.
3. Cover and leave somewhere warm for about 1 hour, until doubled.

Shape and bake
4. Beat the filling butter, brown sugar, cinnamon, cardamom and salt until smooth.
5. Roll the dough to a 40 × 30 cm rectangle, spread the filling to the edges, roll up from the long side and cut into 12.
6. Set the buns cut side up in a buttered 9x13 pan, cover and rise 45 minutes.
7. Bake at 200 °C / 400 °F for 22–25 minutes, until deep golden.
   tip: If the tops brown before the middles are done, cover loosely with foil for the last 5 minutes.

Glaze
8. Melt the butter over medium heat and keep swirling until it smells nutty and the solids turn brown. Cool 5 minutes.
9. Whisk in the icing sugar, milk and vanilla, and spread over the buns while they are still warm.

### 2. `weeknight-dal`

- **Title:** Weeknight Dal with Tarka
- **Owner:** Maria · **Visibility:** Public · **Photo:** p1
- **Description:** Red lentils simmered soft, finished with a sizzling spiced ghee. Thirty minutes, one pot and a small pan.
- **Measure:** `10 min prep · 35 min cook · Serves 4 · Makes about 1.8 L`
- **Tags:** vegetarian, weeknight, lentils
- **Stats:** No adaptations · 6 saves · 2 versions
- **Versions:** `v2` Wed 4 Nov "Double the garlic in the tarka"; `v1` Sun 4 Oct "First version"

Dal
- 1 cup (200 g) red lentils, rinsed
- 4 cups (1 L) water
- ½ tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp fine salt

Tarka
- 3 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- ½ tsp black mustard seeds
- 1 small onion, thinly sliced
- 6 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 green chilli, slit
- ¼ tsp chilli flakes

To finish
- ½ lemon, juiced
- small handful coriander, chopped
- plain yogurt, to serve

Method
Dal
1. Simmer the lentils, water and turmeric, half covered, for 20–25 minutes until completely soft.
2. Whisk until creamy and season with the salt. Loosen with a splash of water if it has thickened.

Tarka
3. Heat the ghee in a small pan until it shimmers. Add the cumin and mustard seeds and wait for the popping.
4. Add the onion and fry 6–8 minutes until golden, then the garlic, chilli and chilli flakes for 1 minute.

To finish
5. Pour the tarka over the dal, squeeze in the lemon and scatter with coriander. Serve with yogurt.

Scaled ×2: Serves **8**, Makes **about 3.6 L** (clay).

### 3. `slow-roasted-tomato-sauce`

- **Title:** Grandmother's Slow-Roasted Tomato Sauce for the Freezer
- **Owner:** Maria · **Visibility:** Only me (lock, "Only you can see this recipe") · **Photo:** none (no-photo band: words only, one 760px column)
- **Description:** Nonna Rosa's Sunday sauce, roasted low instead of stirred for hours. Makes enough to fill the freezer for winter.
- **Measure:** `15 min prep · 3h cook · Serves 8 · Makes 3 jars`
- **Tags:** sauce, make-ahead, freezer
- **Stats:** No adaptations · No saves · 5 versions (private: nobody else can save or adapt it)
- **Versions:** `v5` Sat 24 Oct "Lower oven to 150 °C, roast longer" · `v4` Sat 17 Oct "Add chilli flakes" · `v3` Sun 11 Oct "Whole garlic head, not 6 cloves" · `v2` Sat 3 Oct "Less sugar" · `v1` Sat 26 Sep "Written down from Nonna's card"

Ingredients (ungrouped)
- 2 kg ripe plum tomatoes, halved
- 1 head garlic, cloves peeled
- ⅓ cup (80 ml) olive oil
- 1½ tsp flaky salt
- 1 tsp sugar
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 1 small bunch basil

Method
1. Heat the oven to 150 °C / 300 °F.
2. Toss the tomatoes, garlic, oil, salt, sugar and chilli flakes in a large roasting tin and turn the tomatoes cut side up.
3. Roast for 3 hours, until collapsed and caramelised at the edges.
4. Blend with the basil until as smooth as you like it.
5. Cool, divide between jars, leaving 2 cm at the top, and freeze for up to 6 months.

### 4. `shakshuka` (visitor case)

- **Title:** Shakshuka
- **Owner:** Dev Patel `@devcooks` · **Visibility:** Public · **Photo:** none (visitor + no-photo card case)
- **Description:** Eggs poached in a cumin-spiced tomato and pepper sauce. Bring bread.
- **Measure:** `10 min prep · 25 min cook · Serves 2` (no Makes; the pair is omitted)
- **Tags:** eggs, brunch, vegetarian
- **Stats:** No adaptations · 3 saves · 1 version (plain text, no version bar)
- **Maria's state on it:** not saved, not adapted (Save unpressed). She cooked it on Sun 18 Oct (personal line: "You cooked this on Sun 18 Oct · Log a cook").

Ingredients (ungrouped)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- ¼ tsp cayenne
- 1 can (400 g) whole plum tomatoes
- ½ tsp salt
- 4 large eggs
- 50 g feta, crumbled
- parsley, to serve

Method
1. Soften the onion and pepper in the oil over medium heat for 8 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and spices and cook 1 minute.
3. Add the tomatoes and salt, crush them with a spoon, and simmer 10 minutes until thick.
4. Make four wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook 5–7 minutes until the whites are set.
5. Scatter with feta and parsley and serve from the pan.
