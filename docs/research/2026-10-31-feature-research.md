# Feature wishlist and competitor gaps — 31 October 2026

Inputs: `docs/ROADMAP.md`, `PRODUCT.md`, then web research. **Reddit could not be read**: reddit.com and old.reddit.com refuse this crawler, so no Reddit thread is cited. Frequency instead comes from vote counts on the public trackers of the two biggest open-source recipe managers, read with the GitHub API on 31 October 2026: Mealie feature-request discussions (upvotes, all 1,372 discussions ranked) and Tandoor issues (thumbs-up). Hacker News comments come from the Algolia API. Votes measure self-hosters, a technical crowd; treat them as a lower bound on mainstream demand, not a survey.

## (a) Top 15 candidates, ranked by user value against build cost

Each entry gives what it is, the demand (with links), who ships it, how versioning makes it better, the size, and whether the roadmap already has it.

1. **Cook mode with step timers.** One toggle: wake lock, bigger type, the current step highlighted, timers parsed from step text. Demand: Mealie "special timer step" [#1573](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/1573) (35 votes); on HN tomblomfield wants "Hands-free voice control and being able to access recipe ingredients and steps without 5 pages of SEO-optimised prose" ([HN 43553031](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43553031)). Ships today: Paprika (keeps the screen on, auto-detects timers, highlights the step; [paprikaapp.com](https://www.paprikaapp.com/)), Mela (several recipes in cook mode, Live Activity timers), NYT Cooking (Cook Mode, 2025), Crouton (several timers). Versioning angle: the session knows which version you are cooking, so turning cook mode off can offer the attempt note (M17). Size S. Roadmap: **M18 + M11**.
2. **Units: metric, US and imperial, plus °C/°F.** A per-viewer setting that converts at render time and is never written as a version. Demand: the most-upvoted Mealie request of all, [#3881](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/3881) (111), plus [#2766](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/2766) (14); Tandoor [#345](https://github.com/TandoorRecipes/recipes/issues/345) (12) and volume-to-weight [#2510](https://github.com/TandoorRecipes/recipes/discussions/2510). Ships today: Paprika, Crouton, Pestle. Versioning angle: diffs compare quantities in the reader's units, so "200 g → 1 cup" stops showing as a change. The gram tables (M12) already do most of the work. Size S–M. Roadmap: the backlog's temperature toggle covers part of it; the unit system is **new**.
3. **"I cooked this" log (attempts).** One tap when you finish cooking: the version, the multiplier, a note, an optional photo, and what you changed. Demand: Mealie recipe versioning [#880](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/880) (25) ("I have a lot of recipes that have slight variation to them based on who I am making it for and maybe the event"), and Mealie [#12](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/issues/12) "Feature: Version Control" (8). Ships today: Fava, Larder and Cooked This log meals ([search summary](https://crumble.me/guides/letterboxd-for-restaurants)). None of them is tied to a recipe's history. Versioning angle: "what I changed" can become a new version or a fork in one step. This is the core loop (see c). Size M. Roadmap: **M17**.
4. **Import from a URL.** Read schema.org JSON-LD and prefill the editor. Demand: asked for in every HN Show HN thread on recipe apps ([7courses](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=2087598)) and on [Cronometer's forum](https://forums.cronometer.com/discussion/comment/9366); Instagram import, Mealie [#3846](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/3846) (18). Ships today: everyone. Blog sites are where import quality differs between apps ([Slickdeals thread](https://slickdeals.net/f/16213756-paprika-recipe-manager-app-40-50-off-windows-mac-android-ios)). Versioning angle: v1 records its source URL, and later edits show as diffs from the original as published. Size M. Roadmap: **M15 stage 3**.
5. **Quantities inline in steps, plus a total amount per ingredient.** Demand: Tandoor's second most-voted issue, [#1325](https://github.com/TandoorRecipes/recipes/issues/1325) (20, "show the full required amount per ingredient"), [#2266](https://github.com/TandoorRecipes/recipes/issues/2266) (9) and [#2200](https://github.com/TandoorRecipes/recipes/discussions/2200). Ships today: Scran and Cooklang put amounts in the step; mainstream apps do not. Versioning angle: once ingredients are linked to steps, a diff can say "step 3 now uses the butter". Size M. Roadmap: **backlog** ("quantities inline in steps").
6. **Print and export (PDF, Markdown, .cook).** Demand: Mealie [#1306](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/issues/1306) (20, open); on HN the PurePlates thread calls export the most important feature ([HN 41698328](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41698328)); Paprika's print output gets praise ([HN 25849698](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=25849698)). Ships today: Paprika and Tandoor; Mealie exports JSON only ([Cooklang comparison](https://cooklang.org/blog/42-tandoor-vs-mealie-vs-kitchenowl/)). Versioning angle: export the full history, which no competitor can. Size S (print) to M (archive). Roadmap: **backlog print + M16**.
7. **Alternative and optional ingredients, with substitutions.** Demand: Mealie [#3717](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/3717) (37); Tandoor [#1962](https://github.com/TandoorRecipes/recipes/discussions/1962) (6) and [#2724](https://github.com/TandoorRecipes/recipes/discussions/2724); allergen flags, Mealie [#6854](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/issues/6854). Ships today: Plan to Eat offers per-ingredient swaps ([help page](https://learn.plantoeat.com/help/instant-ingredient-suggestions)); Samsung Food+ does AI swaps. Versioning angle: show swaps backed by evidence, e.g. "4 forks replace butter with oil", counted from fork diffs. Size M. Roadmap: **new**.
8. **A recipe as an ingredient (sub-recipes).** Pie crust, stock, sauces. Demand: Mealie's second most-upvoted request, [#1761](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/1761) (101). Ships today: none of Paprika, Crouton, Pestle or AnyList claim it; whether Tandoor's step recipes do is unverified. Versioning angle: the link pins a version ("uses Crust v2; v3 available, see diff"), the way a lockfile pins a dependency. Size M–L. Roadmap: **new**.
9. **Variants grouped under one parent.** Show a recipe's forks on its page instead of as separate recipes cluttering the feed. Demand: Mealie [#880](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/880) asks for exactly this: "point them to a parent recipe… When you go to the parent recipe it would show you all the child recipe available." Ships today: nobody; Fork's `parentId` already stores the link. Size S. Roadmap: **backlog** (fork count opens a list of forks) and M5.
10. **Household and friend sharing.** Demand: on HN, "My only wish is they would implement a "family" option where I could easily share my library of recipes" ([HN 25849698](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=25849698)) and "If I had 1 request it would be a way to share recipes with friends" ([HN 29164882](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=29164882)); Mealie households [#2134](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/2134) (38) and [#2456](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/2456) (16). Ships today: Pestle Households, AnyList, Crouton through iCloud. Versioning angle: a shared library where every edit is attributed and reversible, so letting a partner edit is safe. Size M. Roadmap: **sharing defaults + M6**.
11. **Photos: several per recipe, per step, and from cooks.** Demand: Tandoor [#1446](https://github.com/TandoorRecipes/recipes/issues/1446) (18) and [#287](https://github.com/TandoorRecipes/recipes/issues/287) (10); Mealie [#4134](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/4134) (23) and [#885](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/885) (19); on HN deng wants "real photos of how the result actually looks" ([HN 43553031](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43553031)). Ships today: Paprika (several images); Untappd and Strava put photos on check-ins. Versioning angle: photos from attempts sit on the version that was cooked, so the gallery shows how the dish changed. Size M. Roadmap: partly M17.
12. **Grocery list from one or more recipes, sorted by aisle.** Demand: Mealie [#1899](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/1899) (41) and [#5240](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/5240) (17); Bring! integration, Tandoor [#911](https://github.com/TandoorRecipes/recipes/issues/911) (18) and Mealie [#790](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/790) (24). Ships today: AnyList (store layouts, Siri and Alexa; [features](https://www.anylist.com/features)), Paprika, ReciMe. Versioning angle: weak; the list remembers the version and multiplier. Size M. Roadmap: **new**; it sits next to M21 pantry.
13. **Nutrition per serving.** Demand: Mealie [#2748](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/2748) (65) and [#3743](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/3743) (37). Ships today: Samsung Food, Cronometer. Versioning angle: the diff shows the nutrition change ("−120 kcal per serving since v2"). Size M. Roadmap: **backlog**, after M12. PRODUCT.md currently excludes macros.
14. **Import from a photo or PDF (OCR).** Demand: Mealie [#1588](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/1588) (59), [#660](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/660) (15) and [#4260](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/4260) (13). Ships today: Crouton, ReciMe (also handwriting), Pestle Pro. Versioning angle: v1 is "as written on Grandma's card", which keeps the family record honest. Size L. Roadmap: **M15 stage 4**.
15. **Meal plan with iCal export.** Demand: Mealie [#850](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/850) (50). Ships today: Paprika, AnyList, Crouton, Pestle, ReciMe, Tandoor. Versioning angle: little. Crowded and far from Fork's position. Size L. Roadmap: **new**; recommend not building soon.

Seen but not ranked: translating a recipe, Mealie [#3918](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/3918) (35) and [#1684](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/1684) (22); searching by excluded tags, [#3396](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/3396) (17); an equipment list, Tandoor [#301](https://github.com/TandoorRecipes/recipes/issues/301) (8); ranges such as "2–3 cloves", Mealie [#2989](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/2989) (13). There is also a scaling bug every scaler should test for: Paprika scales "1 large onion… about 2 cups" to "2 large onion" and leaves the cups unchanged ([HN 25849830](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=25849830)).

## (b) Five gaps no popular app fills

1. **The cook log tied to the version.** Meal-log apps (Fava, Larder, Cooked This) do not version recipes, and recipe managers do not log cooks. No app answers "which version did I make last time, and did the change help?". The versioning request is still an open discussion in Mealie ([#880](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/880)).
2. **Substitutions with evidence behind them.** NYT Cooking's public notes are unstructured free text, and AI swaps are guesses. Nobody counts what real cooks actually changed. Fork's fork diffs can count it.
3. **Sub-recipes pinned to a version.** The top Mealie request after unit conversion, with no change tracking anywhere.
4. **A cook mode that works with a screen reader on the web.** A 2026 test found "Screen readers read the entire ingredient card before reaching step one, which is not how a cook works" and that the category scores poorly on screen readers ([Recipy](https://recipyapp.com/blog/ai-cooking-blind-low-vision-2026)). The one app built for blind cooks is iOS-only and voice-first ([APH](https://aphconnectcenter.org/technology/accessible-cooking-apps-for-blind-and-low-vision/)).
5. **Formatting for neurodivergent cooks** (compound steps split into single actions, prep grouped first, actions in bold) exists only in niche apps: Scran, [Cooktus](https://www.informationisbeautifulawards.com/showcase/7305-cooktus-recepices-your-don-t-have-to-read), and a [UW thesis](https://minds.wisc.edu/items/c95e5e3c-6e21-4a3b-a047-363db4f0ac4d/full). No mainstream app offers it as a reading option.

## (c) Social and versioning concept sketch

1. The atom is a **cook**: a recipe, a version, a multiplier, an optional photo, one line of note, and optional tweaks. It is Strava's activity, Letterboxd's diary entry and Untappd's check-in.
2. Tweaks are a structured diff against the version cooked. "Save as my take" turns them into a fork, and the fork credits the cook that produced it.
3. The feed shows friends' cooks, not new recipes. Each card is the photo plus the diff chip ("v3 · −sugar, +miso"), so the diff is the thing people share.
4. Kudos is one tap, the way Strava designed it to be easier than a comment ([Strava design](https://medium.com/strava-design/understanding-how-athletes-use-the-feed-aae986eaa818)). Comments come later (M3).
5. A recipe page gains "cooked 14 times · 3 takes", which opens the cooks and the forks as one timeline.
6. Your profile is a diary grid, as on Letterboxd: re-cooks count, and there is a year in review ("you made 41 recipes; most-changed: focaccia, 6 versions").
7. Ranking by comparison, as on Beli: after a cook, ask "better or worse than last time?". It is cheaper than stars and directly useful to the cook ([Beli](https://startupsignals.substack.com/p/beli-your-food-diary-and-dining-advisor)).
8. Trends from data Fork already holds: most forked this week, most cooked, and the most common tweak per recipe.
9. A goal on the Goodreads model, "cook 52 new recipes this year", opt-in. No streak guilt by default.
10. This answers the Recipes / My Recipes / Favorites question: Feed (friends' cooks), Cookbook (yours, your takes and saved recipes), Diary (your cooks).

## (d) Accessibility and reaching more people

- Order steps first for screen readers: a skip link to the method, each step a list item, and the current step announced in cook mode through `aria-live` ([Recipy](https://recipyapp.com/blog/ai-cooking-blind-low-vision-2026)).
- Every control labelled; no images that carry ingredient text; no focus traps in dialogs.
- Read steps aloud: `speechSynthesis` in cook mode, with "next" and "repeat". Voice commands are optional and later.
- A large-type mode beyond M18's one-step bump: 150% and 200% sizes, and a layout that holds at 200% zoom.
- A reading option that splits compound steps into single actions and bolds verbs and amounts (gap 5); an optional dyslexia-friendly typeface and wider line spacing.
- Pair every colour cue with a text or time cue: ticks, diff colours, doneness ("until golden, about 8 min").
- Metric, US and imperial units, °C/°F, and locale-aware decimals and fractions (candidate 2). Long run: translation (Mealie [#3918](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie/discussions/3918)).
- Allergen flags derived from ingredient names, plus substitutions (candidate 7). Always label them as estimates.
- Print view and offline reading for kitchens without signal; a PWA cache of recently viewed recipes (M14).
- Tap targets of at least 44px for wet or floury hands; cook mode advances by keyboard and by tapping anywhere on the screen.
