# Design System: Fork

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Annotated Cookbook"**

Fork looks like a cookbook that has been cooked from: cream paper, a serif for the recipe names, and a cook's notes in the margin. The notes are the product. Identifiers (version ids, handles) and diffs are set in a monospace face, the way a pencil annotation differs from the printed page, so a reader always knows which marks are the recipe and which are its history. Everything else recedes: one dark surface (the espresso navbar), one text accent (clay), one action colour (marigold).

Density is editorial rather than app-like. Body text sits at 16px with generous leading, the page has one 1200px column with 24px gutters (18px on a phone), and recipe cards are content on the paper, not tiles. Surfaces separate by a step in tone, never by shadow. The system is warm (paper, clay, marigold), exact (tabular figures, one focus ring, 8px rhythm) and simple (three faces doing three jobs, two button styles, one chip). Its colour should be decently vibrant: the owner finds the current clay and marigold a little dull and wants more life in them, short of anything neon.

Confirmed rejections: no gradients, no glass, no shadows on resting elements, no colour other than clay and marigold outside the diff.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Cream paper ground with one espresso bar; nothing else is dark.
- Bitter for names and titles, Source Sans 3 for everything read, JetBrains Mono for identifiers and diffs only.
- Clay is the only coloured text; marigold is the only fill.
- Depth is tonal: paper, lifted paper, clay tint, espresso.
- Version chips, bylines and diffs are first-class marks, set in mono.

## Colors

A paper-and-ink palette with two warm accents and a semantic pair for diffs.

### Primary

- **Clay** (#b23c00): the only text accent. Page titles (h1), links, recipe names on hover, version numbers in card meta, step numbers. Hover state for a clay link is **Clay Deep** (#6e2500, `--color-accent-hover`; 9.85:1 on paper, 10.72:1 on lifted paper, 1.84:1 below clay, so the hover reads as a change; clay's hue at 22% lightness, deepened from #8f3000 on 10 November 2026, Spencer: "it's not a very different color right now"), never a lighter clay.

### Colour pass, 28 October 2026

Clay and marigold were raised in chroma from #a4512b and #e8a83a: same hues, same lightness, same paper. Clay now sits at the sRGB chroma limit for its lightness. If it reads loud on link-heavy pages, step it back to #af470b and leave the marigold.

### Named Rules

**The Dynamic Is Clay Rule.** Anything the reader changed on the page and that is not stored reads clay: the serves number once it differs from the stored value, every scaled quantity and its weight, and whatever the next live control produces. Clay here means "an estimate you asked for", not a link; the hover stays ink, and stored values stay ink (decided 31 October 2026, Spencer: "dynamic items should be represented with red – serving items, scaled serving ingredients, etc"). The control the reader edits stays ink; only its outputs read clay (Spencer, the same day: "the red doesn't apply to items that are the thing that causes the other changes").

**The Clay Is Text Rule.** Clay never fills a shape, with one named exception: the editor's audience radios take clay as their accent-color (decided 29 October 2026 for the checkbox they replaced). It colours titles, links and version marks. Its hover is Clay Deep.

**The One Direction Rule** (decided 9 November 2026, Spencer: "inconsistency on mouseover, profile turns black and title turns red; already brick links could turn a deeper red"). A hover moves text one step: clay text darkens to Clay Deep; ink text turns clay; grey text (Ink 2 or Ink 3) may turn ink, as the back link and the Cookbook segments do (Spencer, 10 November 2026: "Grey to ink is fine"). Clay text never turns ink on hover; `app.css.test.ts` sweeps every stylesheet for a hover that does.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bitter (with Georgia, serif), weights 500 and 600 **Body Font:** Source Sans 3 (with -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif), weights 400, 500, 600 and 400 italic **Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono (with Fira Code, monospace), weights 400 and 600

**Character:** A sturdy slab serif for the names, a plain humanist sans for the reading, and a monospace for the marks a cook makes on the page. Figures are tabular everywhere so quantities and grams line up.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (500, 2.5rem, 1.15, clay): page h1. Recipe titles, "Recipes", "Search", the profile handle. One clamp on every page, `clamp(1.75rem, 1.2rem + 1.8vw, 2.5rem)`, so phones get about 1.75rem (29 October 2026; no page sets its own h1 size, the landing hero included: its 56px and 44px went at the end of that day, and its lede sits on the ramp at 1.0625rem).
- **Byline** (600, 1rem, 1.5, ink, Source Sans): "By @author · forked from Parent" under a recipe's title; the handle mono and clay one notch under the line's size (0.9375em).
- **Description** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.5, ink, Source Sans): a recipe's description under its byline, plain body text.
- **Headline** (500, 1.75rem, 1.25, ink): h2 on pages that need a second serif level ("Recipes worth forking").
- **Title** (500, 1.0625rem, 1.25): recipe card names in Bitter. Clay on a no-photo panel, ink on a photo card.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.65, ink): running text, ingredients, steps. Descriptions at 1.0625rem in Ink 2. Measure stays under about 72 characters, except method steps, which run the full column (28 October 2026; a 68ch cap was tried on 29 October and read as too narrow).
- **Sans label** (600, 0.75rem, 0.05em, uppercase, Ink 3): the page's one uppercase label: section eyebrows ("Ingredients", "Method", "Recent"), h4, form labels and legends, and the recipe page's phone section tabs at 0.8125rem. Raised from 0.6875rem with 0.08em tracking on 31 October 2026 (Spencer: modestly bigger, tighter tracking). Ink 3 is 4.91:1 on paper and 5.35:1 on lifted paper, the pressed tab's ink 16.14:1, all over AA's 4.5:1. The recipe page's measure is not a label; see Version note.
- **Mono** (400, 0.75rem, 1.5): identifiers only, by the Pencil Rule (Spencer, 31 October 2026): version ids, handles, diff rows and the compare page. On the recipe page mono is spent on the version ids in the bar under the hero (the chip and the history rows' numbers) and the byline's handle, one notch under the line's size like every username; the bar's note, history messages and controls, and the measure, are sans. An identifier or syntax token inside a sans line is a `.mono-id` span or a `<code>`, 0.9375em of the line. Gram weights are not mono: they sit inside a sans ingredient line at the line's own size, in Ink 3, so the strike runs through them as one line (decided 29 October 2026). The recipe page's tag line uses 1rem (decided 29 October 2026); step numerals are Bitter 1.125rem in clay. Diff and compare surfaces use 0.875rem. Card version marks use 600 at 0.6875rem.
- **Also in use** and on the ramp: 0.8125rem (card meta, byline, numerals), 0.875rem (the navbar action, diff, tag options), 0.9375rem (buttons, card body, the auth subtitle), 1rem (navbar links), 1.375rem (the wordmark). Nothing sits off the ramp (29 October 2026: 0.9rem, 0.95rem, 1.1rem, 1.5rem, 1.625rem, 2rem and 2.125rem were snapped to their nearest step).
- **Avatar** (the profile header): a 72px circle of clay tint with the handle's initial in Bitter 500 clay at 1.75rem; 56px and 1.375rem on a phone, with a 1px transparent border inside that size so forced colours keep the circle. The no-photo panel's treatment, for the same reason: there is no photo.
- **Recipe photo** (the title band): 4:3 with 6px corners (capped at 300px tall when stacked), inset in the band; beside the header at 1024 and up, last in the band below that (see Title band).

### Named Rules

**The Pencil Rule.** Mono only for identifiers (Spencer's decision, 31 October 2026). JetBrains Mono sets version ids (v3, "v2 → v3", the chip, the numbers in the history rows), author handles, comparison content (the diff and the compare page's selects and cards) and literal syntax tokens (`tip:`, `warning:`, `substitution:` as `<code>` inside the otherwise sans steps hint). Everything else is the body face, Source Sans: the recipe, dates, the version bar's note, history messages, "N versions" toggle and "Back to vN" and "Compare with vN" links, every editor field, the editor's header line except its version ids, the scaler's multiplier and the profile stats. An identifier inside a sans line is a mono span one notch under it (0.9375em: `.mono-id`, or `<code>` for syntax). Usernames are identifiers and are set in mono like version ids: clay, one notch under the line they sit in (0.9375em, because JetBrains Mono runs optically larger than the sans and serif beside it; decided 29 October 2026), so the byline reads "By" sans, "@author" mono, "· forked from Parent" sans with the parent title a clay link. The fork count lives in the stats row under it (31 October 2026), and a fork names its parent but not the parent's author (one click away); there is no separate attribution line (decided 29 October 2026). The byline's sans exception, decided earlier on 29 October 2026, was withdrawn the same day; the version ids stay mono in the bar under the hero. One place a handle is not mono: the profile page's h1, which is a page title and takes the Display face, Bitter in clay, like every other h1 (decided 29 October 2026).

**The One Clay Line Rule.** Clay running text is the h1 and links. Headings below h1 are ink.

## Layout

One centred column, `max-width: 1200px`, with 24px side gutters on desktop and 18px on phones (`.page`, `.page-inner`). Spacing follows an 8px scale (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64); Tailwind `p-5` to `p-8` are mapped onto the 24, 32, 48 and 64 steps.

Discover (decided 9 November 2026): the lead card and the Recent changes row stay while filtering and draw from the filtered set, so every filter (query, `@handle`, tags, Friends) narrows all three: the lead is the set's most-saved recipe of the last 7 days, else its newest. Every filter is ANDed, two tags included: a recipe must carry both. The lead has no "Open recipe" line; its title link covers the card. On the lead and every grid card the cook (avatar and handle) is one link to their profile, as wide as the two, 24px tall at desktop and 44px on a phone, above the recipe link and before it in Tab order; the two are siblings, never nested. In a Recent changes card the version id is mono at 0.9375em of the title (mono's taller x-height, so it reads the title's size; the recipe page's chip stays 0.75rem, being a chip, not a word in a line) and the handle sits at the message's own size.

Profile relation (decided 9 November 2026): no ⋯ menu on a profile (the Friends feed keeps its own). Friends reads "Friends ✓" as a plain ink text button in the stats line's type, with a Block ghost beside it that turns Diff Remove red on hover and focus. Each opens a two-step confirm in place of its trigger, on the same line (the shared `ConfirmStep`): a red-outline Remove or Block, a ghost Cancel, then "Remove @handle as a friend?" or the block sentence beside them; Escape or Cancel closes it and hands focus back. Home (decided 9 November 2026): there is no separate landing page. Signed out, `/` renders Discover in that same column; signed in, it redirects to `/cookbook`. Search's "All recipes" link goes to `/discover`, where the tag tabs' All goes.

Phones are composed on purpose but never override the desktop and iPad layout: desktop is designed first, then the phone gets its own crop and order.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat, by tonal step. Paper, lifted paper, clay tint and espresso are the four surfaces, and a 1px rule separates what tone cannot. No element at rest carries a shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Raised** (`box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(26, 23, 20, 0.12)`): the open tag list, the one true overlay.

### Named Rules

**The Flat-At-Rest Rule.** Surfaces are flat. A shadow appears only on something that truly floats above the page: a dialog, a menu. The open tag list is the one menu that also joins its box: flush under the search box with one shared edge, square where they meet (6px corners stay at the top of the box, 8px at the foot of the list), the list's Rule 2 sides matching the box's, the shadow clipped at the join, and the focus ring running around box and list together (decided 29 October 2026).

## Shapes

Small, consistent corners: 4px on chips and icon buttons, 6px on buttons, inputs and card photos, 8px on cards and dialogs, 6px on the version bar, pills only on tags. Borders are 1px in Rule or Rule 2. Recipe cards have no box at all; the photo or the clay-tint panel (4:3) is the only shape. Photos are cropped, never letterboxed.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** set version ids, handles and diff text in JetBrains Mono, and nothing else (31 October 2026); dates, the version bar's note and links, and the editor's fields are sans. Gram weights stay sans inside their ingredient line; the card's time and fork count are sans.
- **Do** say "Log in" for signing in and "Log out" for its opposite, everywhere: the navbar, the login page's tab, subtitle and button, and every link to it. "Sign up" is the other verb, and "Sign in" beside it differs by two letters. The confirm-email page's way on reads "Log in" too.
- **Do** end a failure the cook can retry with "Try again.", never "Please try again.", and refuse an action on someone else's recipe with "Not authorized" (31 October 2026).
- **Do** keep clay for titles, links and version marks, and marigold for the one action.
- **Do** separate surfaces with a tone step or a 1px rule; reserve shadows for dialogs.
- **Do** keep body text at 16px, ink, with a measure under about 72 characters.
- **Do** compose desktop and iPad first; give the phone its own crop and order without reshaping the desktop.
- **Do** hold the layout when a recipe has no photo; the clay-tint panel is the fallback.

### Don't:

- **Don't** add a second dark surface, a gradient, a blur or a glow.
- **Don't** fill anything with clay (the editor's checkbox is the one exception), or colour text marigold.
- **Don't** put a shadow on a card, strip or button at rest.
- **Don't** invent proof: no ratings, counts, testimonials or press.
- **Don't** let the version UI crowd the recipe; where it harms reading, reduce it on that surface rather than the whole system.
