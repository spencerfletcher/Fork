# Design System: Fork

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Annotated Cookbook"**

Fork looks like a cookbook that has been cooked from: cream paper, a serif for the recipe names, and a cook's notes in the margin. The notes are the product. Identifiers (version ids, handles) and diffs are set in a monospace face, the way a pencil annotation differs from the printed page, so a reader always knows which marks are the recipe and which are its history. Everything else recedes: one dark surface (the espresso navbar), one text accent (clay), one action colour (marigold).

Density is editorial rather than app-like. Body text sits at 16px with generous leading, the page has one 1200px column with 24px gutters (18px on a phone), and recipe cards are content on the paper, not tiles. Surfaces separate by a step in tone, never by shadow. The system is warm (paper, clay, marigold), exact (tabular figures, one focus ring, 8px rhythm) and simple (three faces doing three jobs, two button styles, one chip). Its colour should be decently vibrant: the owner finds the current clay and marigold a little dull and wants more life in them, short of anything neon.

Confirmed rejections: no gradients, no glass, no shadows on resting elements, no colour other than clay and marigold outside the diff.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Cream paper ground with one espresso bar; nothing else is dark.
- Bitter for names and titles, Source Sans 3 for everything read, JetBrains Mono for identifiers and diffs only.
- Clay is the only coloured text; marigold is the only fill.
- Depth is tonal: paper, lifted paper, clay tint, espresso.
- Version chips, bylines and diffs are first-class marks, set in mono.

### Named Rules

**The Dynamic Is Clay Rule.** Anything the reader changed on the page and that is not stored reads clay: the serves number once it differs from the stored value, every scaled quantity and its weight, and whatever the next live control produces. Clay here means "an estimate you asked for", not a link; the hover stays ink, and stored values stay ink (decided 31 October 2026, Spencer: "dynamic items should be represented with red – serving items, scaled serving ingredients, etc"). The control the reader edits stays ink; only its outputs read clay (Spencer, the same day: "the red doesn't apply to items that are the thing that causes the other changes").

**The Clay Is Text Rule.** Clay never fills a shape, with one named exception: the editor's audience radios take clay as their accent-color (decided 29 October 2026 for the checkbox they replaced). It colours titles, links and version marks. Its hover is Clay Deep.

**The Hover Rule** (decided 11 November 2026, Spencer, options 5A overall with 6B for text controls; it replaces the One Direction Rule of 9 and 10 November). A hover is one step of colour, plus an underline on words that act:

| Element | At rest | On hover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recipe title (cards, Discover's lead and changes) | ink | clay, no underline |
| Handle (`Username`, `.handle`), back link (`.back-link`) | its own colour (clay; Ink 3) | the same colour, underlined |
| Text button and arrow link: Replace, Remove, Reset, "Explore Discover →" (`.text-btn`, `.arrow-link`) | clay, plain | Clay Deep, underlined (`.text-btn--ink`: ink to clay, underlined; Clear ticks: Ink 3 to ink, underlined) |
| An action inside a sentence: "Clear search" beside "No recipes match 'tikka'" (`.inline-action`) | clay, underlined | Clay Deep, still underlined |
| Ghost button, menu row, tab-bar item, the ⋯ trigger (`.more-button`) | its surface | the band |
| Tag tab | Ink 2; the selected tab ink with a clay underline | the inactive label turns ink |

Spencer, 11 November 2026: "Use the same 1px underline with roughly 3px offset throughout. Give keyboard focus a distinct visible outline." The underline is one definition in app.css (1px thick, 3px below the words); every underlined word takes it, so changing it is one line. It underlines words only, never an icon inside the control (a text button is a flex row, so the line stops at the words), and it moves nothing. Focus stays the 2px clay outline at a 2px offset, distinct from the line. "Call 6 settles Reset. It gets the same hover treatment as Replace and Remove. At rest, the underline beneath the editable serving count keeps its meaning. Clear search gets the exception because it sits alongside sentence text and needs to be recognizable as an action on touch." The word hovers sit inside `@media (hover: hover)`, so a tap on a phone leaves nothing underlined. An inline link in a line of names (a byline's parent, a recipe named in the Friends feed or a Journal entry, the tags line, the version bar) is not a sentence action: it stays clay, Clay Deep on hover. Clay text never turns ink on hover. `app.css.test.ts` enforces it: one underline definition, a text control whose hover changes colour alone fails, an underlined title fails, and no hover turns clay text ink.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bitter (with Georgia, serif), weights 500 and 600 **Body Font:** Source Sans 3 (with -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif), weights 400, 500, 600 and 400 italic **Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono (with Fira Code, monospace), weights 400 and 600

**Character:** A sturdy slab serif for the names, a plain humanist sans for the reading, and a monospace for the marks a cook makes on the page. Figures are tabular everywhere so quantities and grams line up.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (500, 2.5rem, 1.15, clay): page h1. Recipe titles, "Recipes", "Search", the profile handle. One clamp on every page, `clamp(1.75rem, 1.2rem + 1.8vw, 2.5rem)`, so phones get about 1.75rem (29 October 2026; no page sets its own h1 size, the landing hero included: its 56px and 44px went at the end of that day, and its lede sits on the ramp at 1.0625rem).
- **Byline** (600, 1rem, 1.5, ink, Source Sans): "By @author · forked from Parent" under a recipe's title; the handle mono and clay one notch under the line's size (0.9375em).
- **Description** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.5, ink, Source Sans): a recipe's description under its byline, plain body text.
- **Headline** (500, 1.75rem, 1.25, ink): h2 on pages that need a second serif level ("Recipes worth forking").
- **Title** (500, 1.0625rem, 1.25): recipe card names in Bitter. Clay on a no-photo panel, ink on a photo card.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.65, ink): running text, ingredients, steps. Descriptions at 1.0625rem in Ink 2. Measure stays under about 72 characters, except method steps, which run the full column (28 October 2026; a 68ch cap was tried on 29 October and read as too narrow).
- **Sans label** (600, 0.75rem, 0.05em, uppercase, Ink 3): the page's one uppercase label: section eyebrows ("Ingredients", "Method", "Recent"), h4, form labels and legends, and the recipe page's phone section tabs at 0.8125rem. Raised from 0.6875rem with 0.08em tracking on 31 October 2026 (Spencer: modestly bigger, tighter tracking). Ink 3 is 4.91:1 on paper and 5.35:1 on lifted paper, the pressed tab's ink 16.14:1, all over AA's 4.5:1. The recipe page's measure is not a label; see Version note.
- **Mono** (400, 0.75rem, 1.5): identifiers only, by the Pencil Rule (Spencer, 31 October 2026): version ids, handles, diff rows and the compare page. On the recipe page mono is spent on the version ids in the bar under the hero (the chip and the history rows' numbers) and the byline's handle, one notch under the line's size like every username; the bar's note, history messages and controls, and the measure, are sans. An identifier or syntax token inside a sans line is a `.mono-id` span or a `<code>`, 0.9375em of the line. Gram weights are not mono: they sit inside a sans ingredient line at the line's own size, in Ink 3, so the strike runs through them as one line (decided 29 October 2026). The recipe page's tag line uses 1rem (decided 29 October 2026); step numerals are Bitter 1.125rem in clay. Diff and compare surfaces use 0.875rem. Card version marks use 600 at 0.6875rem.
- **Also in use** and on the ramp: 0.8125rem (card meta, byline, numerals), 0.875rem (the navbar action, diff, tag options), 0.9375rem (buttons, card body, the auth subtitle), 1rem (navbar links). Nothing sits off the ramp (29 October 2026: 0.9rem, 0.95rem, 1.1rem, 1.5rem, 1.625rem, 2rem and 2.125rem were snapped to their nearest step).
- **Avatar** (the profile header): a 72px circle of clay tint with the handle's initial in Bitter 500 clay at 1.75rem; 56px and 1.375rem on a phone, with a 1px transparent border inside that size so forced colours keep the circle. The no-photo panel's treatment, for the same reason: there is no photo.
- **Recipe photo** (the title band): 4:3 with 6px corners (capped at 300px tall when stacked), inset in the band; beside the header at 1024 and up, last in the band below that (see Title band).

### Named Rules

**The Pencil Rule.** Mono only for identifiers (Spencer's decision, 31 October 2026). JetBrains Mono sets version ids (v3, "v2 → v3", the chip, the numbers in the history rows), author handles, comparison content (the diff and the compare page's selects and cards) and literal syntax tokens (`tip:`, `warning:`, `substitution:` as `<code>` inside the otherwise sans steps hint). Everything else is the body face, Source Sans: the recipe, dates, the version bar's note, history messages, "N versions" toggle and "Back to vN" and "Compare with vN" links, every editor field, the editor's header line except its version ids, the scaler's multiplier and the profile stats. An identifier inside a sans line is a mono span one notch under it (0.9375em: `.mono-id`, or `<code>` for syntax). Usernames are identifiers and are set in mono like version ids: clay, one notch under the line they sit in (0.9375em, because JetBrains Mono runs optically larger than the sans and serif beside it; decided 29 October 2026), so the byline reads "By" sans, "@author" mono, "· forked from Parent" sans with the parent title a clay link. The fork count lives in the stats row under it (31 October 2026), and a fork names its parent but not the parent's author (one click away); there is no separate attribution line (decided 29 October 2026). The byline's sans exception, decided earlier on 29 October 2026, was withdrawn the same day; the version ids stay mono in the bar under the hero. One place a handle is not mono: the profile page's h1, which is a page title and takes the Display face, Bitter in clay, like every other h1 (decided 29 October 2026).

**The One Clay Line Rule.** Clay running text is the h1 and links. Headings below h1 are ink.

## Layout

One centred column, `max-width: 1200px`, with 24px side gutters on desktop and 18px on phones (`.page`, `.page-inner`). Spacing follows an 8px scale (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64); Tailwind `p-5` to `p-8` are mapped onto the 24, 32, 48 and 64 steps.

Discover (decided 9 November 2026): the lead card and the Recent changes row stay while filtering and draw from the filtered set, so every filter (query, `@handle`, tags, Friends) narrows all three: the lead is the set's most-saved recipe of the last 7 days, else its newest. Every filter is ANDed, two tags included: a recipe must carry both. The lead has no "Open recipe" line; its title link covers the card. On the lead and every grid card the cook (avatar and handle) is one link to their profile, as wide as the two, 24px tall at desktop and 44px on a phone, above the recipe link and before it in Tab order; the two are siblings, never nested. In a Recent changes card the version id is mono at 0.9375em of the title (mono's taller x-height, so it reads the title's size; the recipe page's chip stays 0.75rem, being a chip, not a word in a line) and the handle sits at the message's own size.

Profile relation (decided 9 November 2026): no ⋯ menu on a profile (the Friends feed keeps its own). Friends reads "Friends ✓" as a plain ink text button in the stats line's type, with a Block ghost beside it that turns Diff Remove red on hover and focus. Each opens a two-step confirm in place of its trigger, on the same line (the shared `ConfirmStep`): a red-outline Remove or Block, a ghost Cancel, then "Remove @handle as a friend?" or the block sentence beside them; Escape or Cancel closes it and hands focus back. Add friend, Request sent, Accept request and Unblock are unchanged.

Home (decided 9 November 2026): there is no separate landing page. Signed out, `/` renders Discover in that same column; signed in, it redirects to `/cookbook`. Search's "All recipes" link goes to `/discover`, where the tag tabs' All goes.

Closing links, 29 October 2026: a link that ends a page or an empty state takes the arrow form ("All recipes →", "Explore recipes →") and the shared `.arrow-link`, which takes `.hit-link`'s hit: 24px at desktop and 44px on a phone by padding pulled back by margin. A profile's owner gets the page's one action, "New recipe →" to `/recipes/new`, after the recipe list (or the empty line); nobody else sees it. It shows only below 768; from 768 the navbar's + New recipe is the one action. Empty-state links ("Clear search", "Clear filters") take the same 24px and 44px hits through `.empty-state a`, and are sentence actions (`.inline-action`, underlined at rest; see the Hover Rule); "Clear filters" keeps the query and clears the tags only. The back link above a page title (`.back-link`) is as wide as its text, not the column, with the same 24px and 44px hits.

Page width, 29 October 2026: every page is the 1200px column with the site gutters (`.page-inner`); the auth card is a card, not a page width. Decided 9 November 2026: Journal and Friends fill it too, so Discover, Cookbook, Journal and Friends share one left and right edge. Page tops, 31 October 2026: every list page that uses `.browse-header` (Search, a tag, a recipe's forks) starts at one spot. Password recovery, 29 October 2026: a "Forgot password?" link under the login card's password field (clay link, 24/44 hits) leads to /forgot-password and /reset-password, both in the auth card, the first headed "Forgot your password?" and the second "Reset password"; on Supabase's own reset email; the sent state does not say whether the address has an account, and its "Didn't get it? The profile's stats line says "changes", not "commits". Page metadata, 11 November 2026: the root layout writes every head tag from the loader's `meta` (`src/lib/seo.ts`); a page never writes its own `<svelte:head>` preview tags. A public recipe previews as itself (title, its description or one composed from the yield, times and first ingredients, cut near 200 characters on a word; its photo as a large card, the site card `/og.jpg` as a small one without) and carries schema.org Recipe JSON-LD at its latest version only. Anything not everyone may see carries its tab title and `robots: noindex` alone, no description, canonical, og, twitter or JSON-LD: a Friends or Only-me recipe even to the friend or owner reading it, a kept copy, any error page (the 404 for a recipe the visitor may not see says only "Recipe not found"), the compare and forks pages of such a recipe, a profile with nothing public, and every signed-in page (Cookbook, Trash, Journal, Friends, Settings, the editors) and emailed-link page. Canonical is the request's origin and path, never the query: `?serves=`, `?cook=`, filters and `?version=` all name the page without them, so an earlier version previews as itself but names the latest as canonical and has no JSON-LD. Recipe data has no `totalTime`: prep and cook can overlap, as the page has no total. A fork's public parent and the outside source are `isBasedOn`. `static/robots.txt` keeps crawlers out of the signed-in areas.

Leaving the email field for the submit button, by focus or by a pointer pressed on it, does not show the email message; the browser's blocked submit shows it, so the button never moves under a first tap. Focus leaving the submit button without a submit shows a held-back email message. Every auth card, the expired reset link included, ends with the switch line to Log in.

Old links to your profile work for 30 days. You can change it twice in 30 days."; a refusal ("That username is taken", the rule, or past the limit "You can change your username again on Mon 14 Dec.", the day by `dayLabel`) sits under the field with `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby` and takes focus. ## Elevation & Depth

Flat, by tonal step. Paper, lifted paper, clay tint and espresso are the four surfaces, and a 1px rule separates what tone cannot. No element at rest carries a shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Raised** (`box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(26, 23, 20, 0.12)`): the editor's open tag list.

Two shadows remain in the app after 11 November 2026: Raised on the editor's open tag list, and a softer one of its own (`0 8px 24px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.08)`) on the Journal form's recipe list. The menus (`Dropdown`: the avatar menu, the Friends feed's ⋯ and the recipe page's ⋯) carry none.

### Named Rules

**The Flat-At-Rest Rule.** Surfaces are flat. A shadow appears only on a list that drops from a field and covers the page under it: the open tag list, the Journal form's recipe list. Menus are flat too: the page's paper inside a 1px Ink 2 edge (Spencer, 11 November 2026, option 4A softened: "keep 4A's paper fill, 1px border, warm band hover, and no shadow. Try a dark warm brown one step lighter than the text ink for the border"); the edge is 8.68:1 on paper and 7.88:1 on the band, and being a border it stays in forced colours. The open tag list is the one menu that also joins its box: flush under the search box with one shared edge, square where they meet (6px corners stay at the top of the box, 8px at the foot of the list), the list's Rule 2 sides matching the box's, the shadow clipped at the join, and the focus ring running around box and list together (decided 29 October 2026).

## Shapes

Small, consistent corners: 4px on chips and icon buttons, 6px on buttons, inputs and card photos, 8px on cards and dialogs, 6px on the version bar, pills on tags and the photo field's buttons. Borders are 1px in Rule or Rule 2. Recipe cards have no box at all; the photo or the clay-tint panel (4:3) is the only shape. Photos are cropped, never letterboxed.

### Chips

- **Version chip:** marigold fill, ink mono 600 text, 0 6px padding, 4px corners, and a 1px transparent outline drawn inside, so it keeps its box in forced colours, where the fill is dropped. Marigold carries no fixed meaning: it is the vibrant accent, spent sparingly on bold things (the primary button, the chip). On the recipe page the chip marks the version being read, so it stays marigold on an older version too; on the compare page it marks the "To" side; the recipe card's version mark is clay text (decided 29 October 2026, Spencer: "marigold only as the version being read is not inherently true, it's just a vibrant accent colour we should use sparingly").

- **Tags:** transparent pill, 1px Rule 2 border, Ink 2 text at 0.75rem 500. Hover fills clay tint and darkens the border.
- **Tag tabs:** plain text at 0.875rem, Ink 2, 2px transparent underline; the current tab is ink, 600, with a clay underline. One component, `TagTabs`, for every tag row: the tag pages link through it (a nav, one current tab, All first), and Search, Discover and Cookbook toggle it through `SearchFilters` (a group named "Filters" of toggle buttons with `aria-pressed`, no All; signed in on Discover and Search the Friends filter leads it). A pressed filter looks exactly like the current tab, and several can be pressed at once (Spencer, 31 October 2026: the pill was dropped). Each toggle holds its 600 width before it is pressed, so a press moves no neighbour. "Clear all" ends the row, in Ink 3, only while a tag is pressed, so a first pick inserts nothing before the tabs; a press keeps keyboard focus and does not scroll the page, and Clear all hands focus to the first tag (the row's 18px of scroll padding keeps a focused tab's ring from being clipped). On a phone every tab reaches 44px by padding above the text, pulled back by the row's margin. In forced colours the idle tabs' transparent underline is set to Canvas, so only the current or pressed tabs keep a line (forced colours would otherwise paint every tab's, marking all of them current).
- **Search box** (Search, Discover and Cookbook, one `SearchFilters`): the placeholder and name read "Search by name or ingredient…", since the server matches title, description and ingredient names, not titles only. Enter or a tag press applies the query. **Ranking and matches** (`textSearch` and `searchMatches`, `src/lib/server/recipeSearch.ts`; roadmap Search quality, 11 November 2026): a recipe matches on its title or on anything any of its versions says (title, description, ingredient names, steps), ranked by where it matched: the exact title, then a title word or the title's start ("chick" finds Chicken Tikka Masala), then an ingredient of the current version, then the rest of the current version (description, method), then only an older version, last. Saves break ties within a rank, then the newest change; popularity never lifts a weaker match. On Search each card whose title does not show the match says where it did, in place of its description and in its style: "Ingredient: fresh ginger" ("Ingredients:" for more), "Description: …", "Method, step 3: …", "In an older version (v2): Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies"; a fragment cut from a longer text starts with "…". Opening a result at its matched version waits for P2 global search. **Empty states** keep what still applies: "No recipes match “tikka” with these filters." offers Clear filters (keeps the query) or Clear search (keeps the tags and Friends); "No recipes match these filters." offers Clear filters; "No recipes match “tikka”." offers Clear search. A new query keeps the tags and Friends. The native × empties the field and re-runs the search on its own, so the results and the URL drop `?q=` while the selected tags stay (only when the URL has a query, so Enter on an already empty box navigates once); it is drawn in the field's ink (a 16px glyph on `currentColor`, 24px hit), not the browser's blue. Everything else with an estimate is weighed: flour, sugar, butter, oil (0.91), honey and syrups, soy, fish and Worcestershire sauce and balsamic (1.07 to 1.09), vanilla (0.88), and the solids near water the density cannot tell, listed in `WEIGHED_NEAR_WATER` (yogurt, sour cream, cream cheese, canned tomatoes, tomato sauce, tahini; `src/lib/server/gramTables.ts`). Cups, pints and metric volumes are weighed whenever the estimate rounds within tolerance; a teaspoon or tablespoon only from `MIN_SPOON_GRAMS` (5 g: a kitchen scale reads in 1 g steps, so under that the spoon is the better measure; `1 tsp salt` is `6 g`, `¼ tsp espresso powder` stays). The estimate is scaled first, then rounded once by the metric steps; a range weighs both ends or stays. A volume with no estimate, or one that does not round, converts to ml as before. Whole-item estimates (`2 large eggs (≈100g)`) are never promoted, and US is unchanged. **What never converts**: across dimensions otherwise (the gram hint stays as it is), teaspoons and tablespoons to another volume (kitchen-universal, as metric cookbooks keep them), quantities already in the cook's system (the author's own numbers, not re-rounded), counts, pinches, sticks, "to taste", unparsed lines, temperatures in the method and pan sizes. **Rounding**: metric in grams or millilitres, to 1 below 50, 5 below 1000, and 10 from 1000, shown as kg or L (`1.36 kg`); US to kitchen fractions, ¼ ⅓ ½ ⅔ ¾ for volumes (and ⅛ below one), ¼ ½ ¾ for weights, in the largest natural unit that fits: quart from 1, cup from ¼, tablespoon 1 to 4, teaspoon up to 3, pound from 1, else ounces (so 15 ml is 1 tbsp and 454 g is 1 lb). A rounded value more than 6% from the exact one does not land; the next unit is tried, and with none the line stays as written (200 ml stays 200 ml in US). **Display**: a converted measure reads clay by the Dynamic Is Clay Rule, and the measure as written follows it in the gram hint's place and tone, Ink 3 in parentheses, read as "written as" to a screen reader, sharing the bracket with the weight on a liquid (`235 ml (1 cup, 239g) heavy cream`); a converted weight and a weighed volume (`280 g (2¼ cups) flour`) drop the gram figure, since the leading measure is already grams. Scaled too, the bracket holds the scaled measure and is clay like a scaled weight. One Ink 3 line under the list, only when a row converted: "Converted to metric; as written in parentheses." or "Converted to US units; …", then, only when a row led with a gram estimate, "Weights from volume measures are estimates." (`WEIGHED_NOTE`, `src/lib/measurement.ts`), then a "Change in Settings" link to the section.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** set version ids, handles and diff text in JetBrains Mono, and nothing else (31 October 2026); dates, the version bar's note and links, and the editor's fields are sans. Gram weights stay sans inside their ingredient line; the card's time and fork count are sans.
- **Do** say "Log in" for signing in and "Log out" for its opposite, everywhere: the navbar, the login page's tab, subtitle and button, and every link to it. "Sign up" is the other verb, and "Sign in" beside it differs by two letters. The confirm-email page's way on reads "Log in" too.
- **Do** end a failure the cook can retry with "Try again.", never "Please try again.", and refuse an action on someone else's recipe with "Not authorized" (31 October 2026).
- **Do** keep clay for titles, links and version marks, and marigold for the one action.
- **Do** separate surfaces with a tone step or a 1px rule; reserve shadows for a list dropped from a field.
- **Do** keep body text at 16px, ink, with a measure under about 72 characters.
- **Do** compose desktop and iPad first; give the phone its own crop and order without reshaping the desktop.
- **Do** hold the layout when a recipe has no photo; the clay-tint panel is the fallback.

### Don't:

- **Don't** add a second dark surface, a gradient, a blur or a glow.
- **Don't** fill anything with clay (the editor's checkbox is the one exception), or colour text marigold.
- **Don't** put a shadow on a card, strip or button at rest.
- **Don't** invent proof: no ratings, counts, testimonials or press.
- **Don't** let the version UI crowd the recipe; where it harms reading, reduce it on that surface rather than the whole system.
